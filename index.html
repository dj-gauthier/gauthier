Hello and welcome! I am a Chief Technology Officer and Co-Founder of ResCon Technologies, LLC, a Clear Align Company. Previously, I was a Professor of Physics at The Ohio State University (OSU) in Columbus, Ohio, USA. I moved to OSU on January 1, 2016. Previously, I was the Robert C. Richardson Professor in the Department of Physics at Duke University, Durham, North Carolina, USA. I have a wide variety of research interests including machine learning, state-space models for natural language processing, quantum key distribution, and dynamics of complex networks.

I enjoy spending time with my family: my partner, Natasha Bowen, who is a Professor of Social Work at The Ohio State University; my son, Leo Gauthier, who graduated from Duke University in 2013 with a major in psychology, his wife Hollie, and my mother Mona Gauthier. My partner and I live in Hilliard, OH, my son and his wife live in London, UK, and my mother lives in Dublin, Ohio. I also enjoy outdoor activities such as hiking, traveling, kayaking at Griggs Reservoir on the Scioto River, and reading.
